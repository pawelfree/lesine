# GPW history acquisition — initial source check

- EODHD documents historical EOD bars and a separate delisted-symbol retrieval path. It says data completeness differs by delisting date; Polish exchange coverage must be checked at symbol level. [EOD API](https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/api-for-historical-data-and-volumes), [delisted stocks](https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/delisted-stock-companies-data-2).
- EODHD documents dividends and splits separately. [Corporate actions API](https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/api-splits-dividends).
- Historical index membership is documented as a separate premium data product for supported indices. Coverage for WIG has not been verified. [Fundamentals API](https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/stock-etfs-fundamental-data-feeds).
- The owner wants full available GPW history stored locally, including active and delisted stocks, corporate actions and historic index composition. Provider access, coverage, redistribution rights and total acquisition cost remain open.
- EODHD's current EOD documentation says the free key exposes only the past year (20 calls/day plus a welcome allowance), whereas paid plans expose full history. A complete local GPW archive therefore cannot be planned around the free tier alone. EOD updates for most exchanges arrive roughly 2–3 hours after close; a daily result must wait for the required source data rather than assume it exists at the GPW closing bell. The vendor also says adjusted closes are recomputed after dividends, which reinforces retaining raw bars, corporate actions and dated snapshots. [EOD documentation](https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/api-for-historical-data-and-volumes).
- The EODHD exchange list identifies Warsaw as `WAR`, and its symbol list documentation describes separate active and `delisted=1` calls to build a historical universe. This is a starting inventory, not proof of complete history since GPW inception. [Exchange list](https://eodhd.com/list-of-stock-markets), [symbol-list API](https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/exchanges-api-list-of-tickers-and-trading-hours).
- EODHD's commodity history endpoint lists `COPPER` as monthly, with a typical 2–3 week publication lag. It cannot serve as a same-day copper input. A daily copper futures/market proxy requires a separately verified symbol, historical coverage and entitlement. [Commodities API](https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/commodities-api-historical-prices-for-oil-gas-metals-agriculture-beta).
- FRED `DGS2` and `DGS10` are daily series, but their US release occurs after GPW close on many dates; a daily Polish signal must use the latest value that was actually released by its cutoff. FRED/ALFRED supports real-time periods for historical data vintages, while exact intraday publication times still require source-level checks. [DGS2](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DGS2/), [DGS10](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DGS10/), [real-time periods](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/docs/api/fred/realtime_period.html).
- The owner accepted two distinct validation outputs: a longer theoretical WIG-index signal test and a shorter execution test on a basket of BETA ETF WIG20TR, mWIG40TR and sWIG80TR. WIG index levels are not themselves a purchasable instrument, and the three-ETF basket is a declared implementation proxy rather than an exact WIG replica. Basket weights, trading-price convention and available historical price quality remain open. [BETA ETF fund list](https://betaetf.pl/en/our-funds).
- The issuer's history says its WIG20TR and mWIG40TR funds started in 2019 and sWIG80TR in 2021. A three-fund basket would therefore have a shorter valid test period than the two older funds and should not be backfilled using index levels under the same execution-test label. [BETA ETF history](https://betaetf.pl/o-nas/).
- EODHD's personal-use guidance says private display and calculations for one's own investing are allowed; its storage guidance says downloaded data can be retained after cancellation within the personal-use restrictions, while fresh API access stops with the plan. This supports the approved local single-user scope, but does not permit sharing the data or outputs with others. [Personal use](https://eodhd.com/help/personal-use), [retention](https://eodhd.com/help/storage), [private AI apps](https://eodhd.com/help/ai-tools).
- EODHD's delisted-data documentation says its symbol-change-history endpoint currently supports only US exchanges. Therefore a Warsaw symbol rename cannot be assumed to have an authoritative EODHD mapping. The archive must preserve ambiguous source identities and record an explicit reconciliation or gap instead of joining histories by ticker text alone. [Delisted and renamed symbols](https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/delisted-stock-companies-data-2).
