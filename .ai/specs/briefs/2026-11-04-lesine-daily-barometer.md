## Problem

After each GPW session, the owner needs one transparent 0–100 market score, D03 posture and suggested equity allocation by 08:30 Europe/Warsaw before the next session when the local Mac and services are running. A missed powered-off session is reconciled on startup and labeled late or blocked. The first v0 rule is a research hypothesis, not a calibrated probability. Later indicators should be generated by registering versioned JSON/DSL definitions without writing one calculator per indicator.

## Agreed direction

- US-2.1: register and validate a restricted, versioned JSON/DSL definition plus versioned D03 policy; an invalid definition never activates and the old version remains research-accessible.
- US-2.2: use the shared deterministic engine and source snapshot known by the 08:30 cutoff; publish the score/posture/allocation or explicit blocked state, never a partial or backdated signal.
- US-2.3: show score history, input/version lineage and corrections; a revision produces a new result, not an overwrite.
- v0 components and exact math are in App Spec §1.4; first operator set must express time-series and historical-universe breadth operations. Main Market ordinary shares, including later-delisted shares, enter breadth; NewConnect does not.
- Capability ladder: new Indicator research module, 11 provisional atomic commits in §4/`app-spec-notes/commit-plan.md`. No existing platform mechanism is claimed. First release needs no visual formula editor.

## Non-goals

- No automated brokerage action, personal holdings tagging, visual formula editor or faithful replica of the proprietary PW formula.
- No calculation when a required input fails the freshness or coverage gate.
