# D03 — The full Investment Intelligence Platform vision stays a later-phase direction, not committed scope

- Date, owner: 2026-10-14, product owner (self)
- Context and the options weighed: `preliminary_spec.md` describes a large, multi-component platform (data platform, feature/factor engine, hypothesis factory, experiment factory, backtesting/validation, strategy management, market regime engine, portfolio intelligence, risk management, recommendations, paper trading, continuous learning, governance). None of it is built, and none of it has been validated against the product owner's actual goal (see the product brief's Vision and Problems sections).
- Decision and why: keep this document as the team's own long-term direction (`[ASSUMPTION]`/`[DOCUMENT]`, not evidence), useful for not losing sight of the big picture, but treat none of it as committed scope. The committed scope is the narrower MVP in D02. Each later phase gets re-evaluated against what the MVP actually learns.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: prevents scope creep into the full architecture before the single-advisor pipeline proves useful. Revisit as each MVP phase completes and the product owner decides what to build next.
- Status: active
