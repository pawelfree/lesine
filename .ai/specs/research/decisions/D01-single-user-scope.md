# D01 — Single-user scope for the MVP

- Date, owner: 2026-10-14, product owner (self)
- Context and the options weighed: whether the MVP needs to support multiple investors/clients (accounts, permissions, multiple portfolios) or just the owner's own use.
- Decision and why: the product owner is the sole user. No multi-user accounts, permissions, or client-facing features in the MVP.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: simplifies the MVP to a single-tenant tool. Revisit if the product owner decides to share the system with other investors or clients.
- Status: active
